Unison privacy policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Unison ("the app") is published by Decision Chain Holdings LLC ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Unison.

The short version

Unison does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. Your singing is analyzed on your iPhone, and everything the app stores stays on your iPhone.

Microphone and voice

Unison uses your iPhone's microphone only while you are doing an exercise (a voice check, a drill or a voice sample), to measure the pitch of your singing in real time. This audio is processed on your device and never uploaded. Your most recent practice attempt is kept in memory only, so you can play it back ("Your take" and "You, in tune"); it is replaced by your next attempt and discarded when you close the exercise. The only recordings Unison saves are your voice sample and your progress clips, described below.

During the voice check (and if you choose to re-record it later), Unison saves a short recording of you singing "ah" (up to about ten seconds). It is used to play exercises back to you in your own voice. The sample is stored only in the app's private storage on your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

Progress clips and before-and-after videos

If you choose to record a progress clip, Unison saves a short recording (a few seconds) of you singing one line, "Happy birthday to you", along with its pitch line and score, so you can compare how you sound over time. Clips are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Unison keeps your first clip and up to eleven more recent ones, deleting older clips automatically; recording again on the same day replaces that day's clip.

If you choose to make a before-and-after video, it is created on your device from your first and latest clips and kept in temporary storage. It leaves your device only if you share it yourself, using the share sheet, to a destination you choose.

Practice data

Unison stores your voice range, voice-check results, practice history, settings and daily singing time on your device so it can adapt your training. This data is never transmitted to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription status using Apple's StoreKit framework on your device.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Unison contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Backups

If iCloud Backup or computer backups are turned on for your device, the app's stored data (including the voice sample and progress clips) may be included in those backups, under Apple's policies and your own settings.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings › Export my data creates a copy of your stored practice data (scores, history and the pitch lines of your progress clips, but no audio) and hands it to the share sheet; nothing is sent unless you choose where it goes.
  • Delete: Settings › Delete all my data permanently removes your voice sample, progress clips, before-and-after videos, results and history from the device. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored. Deleting data doesn't cancel a subscription; you can do that in your Apple Account settings.
  • Microphone: you can turn off microphone access at any time in iOS Settings › Unison. The app can't run exercises without it.

Children

Unison is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example because we add optional cloud features, we will update this page and the effective date and, where required, ask for your consent in the app first.
